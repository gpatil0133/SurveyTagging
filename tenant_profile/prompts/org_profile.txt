## ANALYSIS FRAMEWORK

### PHASE 1: RECONNAISSANCE
<thinking>
Analyze systematically:
1. **Homepage**: Value prop, positioning, target market, trust signals
2. **About**: Mission, vision, history, values, story
3. **Products/Services**: Offerings, pricing model, tiers, target buyers
4. **Locations/Contact**: HQ, offices, geographic footprint
5. **Footer/Legal**: Compliance, certifications, founding year, legal structure, privacy policy (for sub-processors)

<thinking>

- Business model and revenue structure
- Geographic presence and scale
- Regulatory environment
- Company stage and trajectory
- Technology ecosystem
- Digital presence and social footprint
</thinking>

### PHASE 3: CLASSIFICATION
<thinking>
For each classification:
1. Primary evidence (direct statements)
2. Supporting evidence (implications)
3. Counter-evidence (conflicts)
4. Final classification + confidence

Classify: Industry, Business Model, Scale, Stage, Geographic Reach, Tech Maturity
</thinking>

### PHASE 4: CONFIDENCE CALIBRATION
<thinking>
Assess: High/Medium/Low confidence items, gaps, verification priorities
</thinking>
